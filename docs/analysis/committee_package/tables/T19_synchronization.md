**Notes.**

- Sync method: robust augmented hierarchical six-group edge matching with piecewise interpolation through gige_time (version 3.2.0-robust-augmented-earlystop), the same for all 24 coupons. Timestamps strictly increasing and no out-of-range or repeated mapped rows in every coupon: yes.
- Raw frames are the whole layer's camera stream shared by a set's six coupons; sync-mapped are those mapped to this coupon's process rows.
- Anchor convention (ledger O11): in July the first eligible frame of a track maps to the track's first powered row (100 % of tracks), on the rising-edge anchor; in September the first frame maps a median 10–14 rows after the start and the last frame 9 rows before the end, on the falling-edge anchor. The convention is campaign-wide and confounded with acquisition day (confound table).
- The track-start and track-end mapping was recomputed here from the characterization files for all 24 coupons; for the 16 A8 calibration members it equals the Patch 22.3.6 audit (96 values). The 8 held-out coupons were not in that audit (it read calibration members only), so their anchor fractions are not available; their start and end mapping follows their campaign.
- Row maps back to frame: share of scored frames whose mapped process row maps back to the same camera frame (22.4.3.2 reading S): 95.8–100.0 %.
